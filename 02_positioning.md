# 02 — Positioning

## The Category You're Creating

Don't position yourself inside an existing category (dental consulting, hygiene CE, practice coaching). **Create a category you can own.**

### Category Name (working): **Hygiene-Led Practice Growth™**

> A practice growth discipline where the hygiene department — not marketing, not new patient flow, not doctor-side case presentation — is treated as the primary engine of revenue, retention, and treatment acceptance.

This is a category land-grab. Today nobody owns the phrase "hygiene-led practice growth." Once your book is out, you do.

---

## The Positioning Statement (use verbatim in bio, talks, sales pages)

> **For the dental practices and hygienists who know the hygiene chair is doing 10% of what it could** — Chairnomics™ is the only **hygiene-led practice growth system** that turns the hygiene appointment into the practice's #1 driver of treatment acceptance, production per hour, and patient retention. Unlike traditional dental consultants who optimize the front desk and doctor schedule, we start where the relationship lives: **the chair**.

---

## The Enemy (every strong brand has one)

You're not against people. You're against **ideas** that have hurt the profession. Naming them clearly makes the brand magnetic.

| The Enemy | What it sounds like | Why it's wrong |
|---|---|---|
| "Hygiene is just a cleaning." | "The doctor does the real dentistry." | Hygiene is the longest, most repeated patient interaction in the practice. |
| "Stay in your lane." | "Don't diagnose, that's the doctor's job." | Co-diagnosis is legal, ethical, and the #1 lever for case acceptance. |
| "We can't double-book hygiene." | "Patients want a leisurely cleaning." | Patients want efficiency and respect. Burnt-out hygienists want sane systems. |
| "Tracking numbers ruins the patient experience." | "We're not a sales team." | Untracked is uncared-for. KPIs *are* patient care made visible. |
| "Hygienists are interchangeable." | "Anyone with a license can do it." | Hygienists are the trust engine; replacing one resets the relationship clock to zero. |

---

## The Promise (the transformation you sell)

A practice that adopts the Chairnomics™ system will, within **90 days**:

1. Lift **hygiene production per hour by 35–60%** without adding patients
2. Raise **same-day treatment acceptance from the chair by 20–40 points**
3. Cut hygienist burnout signals (call-outs, complaints, turnover risk) by **at least half**
4. Implement **double hygiene or accelerated hygiene** in at least one column without dropping clinical quality
5. Install a **mentor cadence** that makes hygienists *want* to stay

These are the public-promise numbers — your book will show the math, the mechanics, and the case studies behind them.

---

## Differentiators (why you, not the other 200 dental consultants)

1. **Built from the chair, not the boardroom.** You're an RDH who built this from inside the operatory. Every script has been said by you to a real patient. Every KPI has been your KPI.
2. **Whole-team frameworks.** Most hygiene CE talks *to* hygienists. Most practice consulting talks *to* doctors. You teach the **handoff choreography** between them — the part that's actually broken.
3. **Efficiency without exploitation.** Double hygiene is one of your specialties — *and* you'll publicly draw the line on when it's wrong. That credibility is rare.
4. **Proprietary methodology with named phases.** Not "best practices." A trademarked system with terminology the industry will start borrowing from you.
5. **Mentor model, not consultant model.** You don't fly in, install, and leave. The brand is built around hygienists mentoring hygienists, with doctors as co-learners.
