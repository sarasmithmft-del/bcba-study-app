# 09 — Trademark Filing Strategy

> **A one-page strategy to hand to a trademark attorney.** Print this and send it. Most attorneys quote $400–$800 per mark + USPTO filing fees of $250–$350 per class.
>
> **Not legal advice.** This is your strategist's recommendation. A licensed trademark attorney must confirm availability and execute the filings.

## What You're Filing

You are building a brand with a multi-layered IP portfolio. The marks below are listed in **priority order** — file the top tier *now*, the second tier within 60 days, and the third tier as the related products go to market.

---

## Tier 1 — File Within The Next 30 Days

These are the foundational marks. They are the brand company name and the two methodologies that the book and the consulting practice both depend on.

### 1. **Chairnomics™**

- **Type**: Fanciful / coined word mark — strongest TM category
- **Filing classes**:
  - **Class 35** — Business consulting services (dental practice management consulting)
  - **Class 41** — Educational services (workshops, CE, certifications, online courses)
  - **Class 16** — Printed publications (the book, workbooks, handouts)
- **Standard character mark** + **design/logo mark** once visual identity is finalized — file the standard character first to lock the word
- **Estimated USPTO fees**: ~$1,050 (3 classes × $350) plus attorney time

### 2. **The C.H.A.I.R. Method™**

- **Type**: Suggestive / acronym mark
- **Filing classes**: Class 35, Class 41, Class 16
- File as: `THE C.H.A.I.R. METHOD` (standard character)
- Note in the application that the acronym stands for *Connect, Hear & Hunt, Assess & Articulate, Invite, Reinforce & Re-Engage*

### 3. **The A.P.E.S. Method™**

- **Type**: Suggestive / acronym mark
- **Filing classes**: Class 35, Class 41
- File as: `THE A.P.E.S. METHOD` (standard character)
- Note in the application: *Assess, Plan, Engage, Sustain*

**Total Tier 1 investment**: roughly **$3,000–$5,000** all-in (USPTO fees + attorney time for 3 marks across 7 class registrations).

## Filing Strategy Notes (for the attorney)

- **File "intent to use" (1B) if you haven't formally launched yet.** This reserves the mark for up to 36 months while you go to market. Then convert to "in use" (1A) with a Statement of Use once Chairnomics is generating revenue.
- **Standard character marks first, design marks second.** Lock the word; the logo comes later when the visual identity is finalized.
- **File the methodology marks (.M. and .A.P.E.S.) as wordmarks with the acronym explained in the application narrative.** Acronyms with explanatory long-forms get cleaner USPTO review.
- **For "Chairnomics," consider the Madrid Protocol** for international protection if you plan to sell into Canada, UK, or Australia within 5 years — file the US application first, then the international extension within 6 months.

## What You're Defending

The trademarks are not vanity. They are the moat. Specifically:

- **Chairnomics** — protects the brand company name, the consulting practice, the certification body
- **The C.H.A.I.R. Method** — protects the methodology in the book + course, prevents copycat "Chair Methods"
- **The A.P.E.S. Method** — protects the consulting product, prevents other consultants from selling "A.P.E.S. consulting"
- **The Mentor Cadence** — protects the retention layer that creates recurring revenue

A competitor can copy a chapter idea. They cannot copy a registered mark. The marks are what make the brand sellable, licensable, franchisable, and eventually acquirable.
