# 05 — Proprietary Terminology Glossary

Every category-defining brand owns words the industry didn't have before. This glossary is **your language**. Use it consistently in book, content, talks, and training. After 12–18 months of consistent use, the industry will start saying these terms — and they'll be yours.

> Convention: **bold** = a coined term you should always use. *italic* = the legacy phrase you're replacing.

---

## I. Big Frame Terms

| Your Term | Replaces | What It Means |
|---|---|---|
| **Chairnomics™** | *"dental consulting"*, *"hygiene consulting"* | The brand. The economics of the chair. The company Sara Smith, RDH built around Hygiene-Led Practice Growth™. |
| **The C.H.A.I.R. Method™** | *"hygiene workflow"*, *"hygiene best practices"* | The 5-phase clinical methodology (Connect, Hear & Hunt, Assess & Articulate, Invite, Reinforce & Re-Engage) hygienists use in the operatory. |
| **The A.P.E.S. Method™** | *"consulting engagement"*, *"practice transformation"* | The 4-phase consulting methodology (Assess, Plan, Engage, Sustain) Sara uses to install C.H.A.I.R. inside a practice. The billable product of Chairnomics. |
| **Hygiene-Led Practice Growth™** | *"hygiene production"*, *"hygiene revenue"* | A practice growth discipline where the hygiene department is treated as the primary engine of practice revenue, retention, and acceptance. |
| **The Sacred 50** | *"the hygiene appointment"* | The 50-minute hygiene visit, reframed as the most valuable patient touchpoint in the practice. |
| **Chairside Equity** | *"rapport"*, *"chairside manner"* | The accumulated trust a hygienist holds with a patient across visits. Compounds like financial equity. |
| **The Trust Window** | (no current equivalent) | The first 90 seconds of an appointment, where trust is set or lost for the entire visit. |

## II. Revenue & Production Terms

| Your Term | Replaces | What It Means |
|---|---|---|
| **The 3 Revenue Streams of the Chair™** | *"hygiene production"* | Direct, Diagnostic, and Retention production — your model. |
| **Direct Production** | (clarifies existing) | Procedures performed by the hygienist (prophy, perio, fluoride, etc.). |
| **Diagnostic Production** | *"doctor production"* | Restorative work that originates as a chairside hygiene observation. The hygienist's contribution to doctor-side revenue. |
| **Retention Production** | *"recall"* | Revenue protected by the hygienist via reattendance, reactivation, and referrals. |
| **HPPH (Hygiene Production Per Hour)** | *"hygiene revenue"* | The single most important hygiene KPI. Industry tracks it loosely; you make it the north star. |
| **Chairside Co-Diagnosis Rate (CCDR)** | (no current equivalent) | % of hygiene appointments that produce at least one doctor-side treatment plan. |
| **The Acceptance Gap** | *"case acceptance shortfall"* | The difference between what the hygienist identified and what the patient accepted. The whole brand exists to close this gap. |

## III. Workflow & Efficiency Terms

| Your Term | Replaces | What It Means |
|---|---|---|
| **Double Hygiene** | *"two-column hygiene"*, *"split hygiene"* | Two hygienists running parallel columns with shared doctor exams. (Yours to define cleanly.) |
| **Accelerated Hygiene** | *"15-minute prophy"* (often misused) | A protocol-driven workflow that completes a quality hygiene visit in a compressed timeframe, when clinically appropriate. |
| **Assisted Hygiene** | *"hygienist with an assistant"* | One hygienist + one trained hygiene assistant running two columns, with explicit role-splits you'll define in the book. |
| **The Choreography** | *"workflow"* | The hygiene visit reframed as a 5-phase dance with the doctor. Choreography is rehearsed, workflows aren't. |
| **The Handoff** | *"the exam"* / *"the doctor check"* | The 60-second transition from hygienist to doctor. The single most leveraged moment of the appointment. |
| **The Re-Entry** | *"the post-op"* | The post-exam re-engagement of the patient by the hygienist after the doctor leaves. |

## IV. Conversation & Language Terms

| Your Term | Replaces | What It Means |
|---|---|---|
| **The Conversation Conversion Ladder™** | *"scripting"*, *"case presentation language"* | Your 5-rung chairside language model. |
| **Co-Diagnosis** | *"selling"*, *"telling"* | A hygienist and patient looking at evidence (camera, x-ray, perio chart) and arriving at the same conclusion together. |
| **The Notice** | *"the find"* | The hygienist's first verbal acknowledgement of a clinical finding. |
| **The Mirror** | *"showing them their teeth"* | The use of intra-oral camera or photo to let the patient see what you see. |
| **The Frame** | *"diagnosis"* | The clinical interpretation the hygienist offers, in plain language. |
| **The Bridge** | *"telling the doctor"* | The verbal handoff that primes the patient and informs the doctor in one sentence. |
| **Chairside Vocabulary** | (no current equivalent) | The 30–50 patient-facing words and phrases the team agrees to use (or avoid) practice-wide. |
| **Word Leaks** | *"bad scripts"*, *"verbal habits"* | Phrases that quietly cost the practice money or trust (e.g. "It's just a small cavity," "We can watch this," "Your insurance won't cover that"). |

## V. Culture & Mentorship Terms

| Your Term | Replaces | What It Means |
|---|---|---|
| **The Mentor Cadence™** | *"team meetings"*, *"1:1s"* | Your 4-tier rhythm: Daily Reset, Weekly Walk-Through, Monthly Mentor Hour, Quarterly Quiet. |
| **The Daily Reset** | *"morning huddle"* | A 5-minute pre-shift micro-check, hygiene-led. |
| **The Quarterly Quiet** | *"performance review"* | A half-day, non-rushed conversation between hygienist and doctor about career, finances, vision. |
| **The Hygiene Lead** | *"lead hygienist"* | A formal role with explicit KPIs, mentor responsibilities, and (importantly) compensation. |
| **The Open** | *"the cleaning"* | The hygienist's role in *opening* the patient to treatment, restated as honored work. |
| **The Close** | *"the exam"* / *"case presentation"* | The doctor's role, restated as the natural continuation of what the hygienist opened. |
| **Mentor Model** | *"management"* | Your operating philosophy. Mentorship is the retention strategy. |

## VI. Practice Health Terms (the KPI vocabulary)

| Your Term | Replaces | What It Means |
|---|---|---|
| **The Hygiene Health Score™** | *"hygiene metrics"* | A composite of HPPH, CCDR, Reattendance Rate, and Same-Day Acceptance, rolled into a single 0–100 number you publish quarterly. |
| **SDA (Same-Day Acceptance)** | (no current equivalent) | % of hygiene-originated findings accepted/scheduled by the patient on the same day of diagnosis. |
| **RAR (Reappointment / Reattendance Rate)** | *"recall rate"* | % of patients who complete their next scheduled hygiene appointment in the measurement period. |
| **Exam Delay Average** | *"doctor running late"* | Average minutes between a requested doctor exam and the doctor entering the operatory. |
| **Missed Break/Lunch Frequency** | *"team burnout signs"* | Count of skipped breaks or lunches per hygienist per week; a key stamina and sustainability KPI. |
| **Acceptance Velocity** | *"case acceptance over time"* | How quickly a patient moves from diagnosis to accepted treatment to scheduled appointment. |
| **The Trust Compound** | (no current equivalent) | The retention/referral revenue that compounds when chairside equity is built over multiple visits. |

| **The Plan Phase** | *"strategy"* | Week 3 — the customized rollout. |

| **The Sustain Phase** | *"ongoing coaching"* | Months 4–12+ — Mentor Cadence™ + KPI rhythm. |

## How to Use This Glossary

1. **In the book**: First mention of any coined term gets a ™ and a 1-sentence definition in-line. End-of-book glossary repeats this list.
2. **On socials**: Pick one term per week as a "term-of-the-week" post. Builds language, builds following.
3. **In speaking**: Every keynote should plant 3–5 terms; audiences will repeat them.
4. **In coaching**: Make clients adopt the language inside their practice. When their team uses your words, you've embedded the brand.
