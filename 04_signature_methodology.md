# 04 — Signature Methodology

This is the **proprietary system architecture** that lives at the center of Chairnomics™. Every chapter of *The Sacred Chair*, every keynote, every course, every certification, and every consulting engagement traces back to this structure.

The brand has **two methodologies** operating at two different levels — and that two-level architecture is one of the most defensible parts of the brand. Most dental consulting firms have one framework (or none). You have two, working together.

# Methodology 1: The C.H.A.I.R. Method™

> A 5-phase clinical system that turns the hygiene chair from a cleaning station into the practice's primary growth engine.

| Letter | Phase Name | What It Is | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C** | **Connect** | The first 90 seconds: trust-set, reset, relationship-set. | Patients decide if they trust you before the bib clips on. |
| **H** | **Hear & Hunt** | Active intake + clinical hunt: medical, social, oral. | Most hygienists clean before they listen. Reverse it. |
| **A** | **Assess & Articulate** | Co-diagnosis, intra-oral camera, narrated findings. | Patients don't accept what they don't *see and understand*. |
| **I** | **Invite** | The transition: from finding to decision, hygienist-led. | The handoff to the doctor is where most acceptance leaks. |
| **R** | **Reinforce & Re-Engage** | Post-op script, recall mechanics, between-visit touchpoints. | The chair lives between appointments, not just during them. |

This is the methodology hygienists learn and use. Every chapter of *The Sacred Chair* (Chapters 4–8) maps to one letter.

> **Pronunciation tip**: When you say "The CHAIR Method™," it lands twice — as an acronym *and* as the literal chair. That double-meaning is brand gold.

# Methodology 2: The A.P.E.S. Method™

> A 4-phase consulting engagement system that installs Chairnomics™ inside a dental practice and sustains it across a year.

**A.P.E.S. = Assess. Plan. Engage. Sustain.**

This is the methodology *Sara* (and eventually certified implementers) uses. It is the *consulting product* of Chairnomics. Practices don't buy "consulting" — they buy an A.P.E.S. engagement. Other consultants don't get "trained" — they become *A.P.E.S. Certified Implementers.*

| Phase | Duration | What Happens | Deliverable to Practice |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Assess** | Weeks 1–2 | Sit in operatories. Audit current chairside conversations. Run the 3-Stream Calculator (Tool 2.1). Calculate the Hygiene Health Score™. Interview each hygienist + doctor. | **The Chairnomics Assessment Report™** — a 15–20 page document with the practice's baseline numbers, observed patterns, and the gap to industry top-quartile |
| **P — Plan** | Week 3 | Build the customized rollout: which C.H.A.I.R. phases this practice needs first, the Double Hygiene Decision Matrix™ output, staffing math, comp adjustments, the 90-day implementation calendar | **The Practice Plan™** — a 10–12 page implementation roadmap, doctor-approved and signed |
| **E — Engage** | Weeks 4–12 | Live operatory training, chairside language drills, role-play workshops, doctor-hygienist choreography rehearsal, KPI tracking activation, weekly check-ins | Weekly progress reports + practice-wide adoption of the 4 hygiene KPIs (HPPH, CCDR, RAR, HSRR) |
| **S — Sustain** | Months 4–12+ | The Mentor Cadence™ (Daily Reset, Weekly Walk-Through, Monthly Mentor Hour, Quarterly Quiet). Monthly KPI review. Quarterly recalibration. Annual practice review. | Ongoing retainer engagement. Practice maintains the gains and compounds them. |

### Why A.P.E.S. Pairs Beautifully with C.H.A.I.R.

The two methodologies fit together like a key and a lock:

- **C.H.A.I.R. is what the hygienist does.** A.P.E.S. is what makes the hygienist *able and willing* to do it consistently across an entire practice.
- **C.H.A.I.R. is taught in a book and a course.** A.P.E.S. is delivered in a contract and a retainer.
- **C.H.A.I.R. has a one-time learning curve.** A.P.E.S. has a recurring revenue model.
- **C.H.A.I.R. ceiling**: one hygienist's chair. **A.P.E.S. ceiling**: every dental practice in North America.

The book sells C.H.A.I.R. to the world. The consulting practice sells A.P.E.S. to the practices who read the book. The certification program sells A.P.E.S. to other consultants who want to do this work. Three revenue streams, one architecture.

## Sub-Frameworks (Tools Inside the Methodologies)

These are the named sub-tools that live inside C.H.A.I.R. and A.P.E.S. They are taught in the book and used in engagements.

### Sub-Framework #1: The 3 Revenue Streams of the Chair™
*Lives inside the **Assess** phase of A.P.E.S. + Chapter 2 of the book.*

| Stream | What It Is | KPI |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct Production** | Hygienist's hands-on procedures | HPPH (Hygiene Production Per Hour) |
| **Diagnostic Production** | Restorative dentistry sourced from the chair | CCDR (Chairside Co-Diagnosis Rate) |
| **Retention Production** | Revenue protected by the relationship | RAR + HSRR (Reattendance + Hygienist-Sourced Referrals) |

### Sub-Framework #2: The Double Hygiene Decision Matrix™
*Lives inside the **Plan** phase of A.P.E.S. + Chapter 9 of the book.*

```
                         Patient Volume Pressure
                              LOW          HIGH
                         ┌──────────────┬──────────────┐
                         │              │              │
       HIGH (1.5+ FTE)   │  Optimize    │  DOUBLE      │
                         │  Solo Flow   │  HYGIENE     │
        Hygienist        │  (Quadrant 1)│  (Quadrant 2)│
        Capacity         ├──────────────┼──────────────┤
                         │  Recruit     │  Accelerated │
       LOW (<1.0 FTE)    │  First       │  / Assisted  │
                         │  (Quadrant 3)│  Hygiene     │
                         │              │  (Quadrant 4)│
                         └──────────────┴──────────────┘
```

- **Quadrant 1 — Optimize Solo Flow**: Tighten C.H.A.I.R. choreography first; don't scale chaos.
- **Quadrant 2 — Double Hygiene**: Two hygienists, two columns, doctor-shared exams.
- **Quadrant 3 — Recruit First**: Hire before scaling; doubling on too few clinicians breaks them.
- **Quadrant 4 — Accelerated / Assisted Hygiene**: One hygienist + one trained assistant running two columns.

Sara's specialty. Taught with the matrix, the staffing math, the schedule templates, and the *cultural* preconditions for each quadrant.

### Sub-Framework #3: The Conversation Conversion Ladder™
*Lives inside the **A — Assess & Articulate** phase of C.H.A.I.R. + Chapter 6 of the book.*

A 5-rung chairside language model. Each rung is a scripted move taking a patient one step closer to "yes."

1. **The Notice**: *"I see something I want to look at more closely with you."*
2. **The Mirror**: *"Here's what I'm seeing. Have you noticed this?"* (intra-oral camera)
3. **The Frame**: *"Here's what I think is going on, and here's what it could mean if we don't address it."*
4. **The Choice**: *"When the doctor comes in, would you like us to talk about options for this?"*
5. **The Bridge**: *"Doctor, when you have a second — [Patient] and I were just looking at #14. They had a great question about it."*

### Sub-Framework #4: The Mentor Cadence™
*Lives inside the **Sustain** phase of A.P.E.S. + Chapter 12 of the book.*

A 4-tier mentorship rhythm that keeps hygienists from leaving practices:

| Cadence | Frequency | Format | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Daily Reset** | Daily, 5 min | Pre-huddle micro-check | Hygiene Lead |
| **The Weekly Walk-Through** | Weekly, 20 min | KPI review + one story | Hygiene Lead + Doctor |
| **The Monthly Mentor Hour** | Monthly, 60 min | Case review, language drills, growth goal | Hygienist + Sara (or A.P.E.S. Certified Implementer) |
| **The Quarterly Quiet** | Quarterly, half-day | Career conversation, financial review, vision | Doctor + Hygienist |

The Mentor Cadence™ is the retention play. It is the part of A.P.E.S. that creates compounding retainer revenue — and the part that keeps practices from regressing 90 days after the consultant leaves.

### Sub-Framework #5: The Hygiene Health Score™
*Lives inside the **Assess** phase of A.P.E.S. + Chapter 11 of the book.*

A composite 0–100 score rolling four KPIs into a single number you publish quarterly. Used as the headline metric in the Chairnomics Assessment Report™ and as the benchmarking number against industry top-quartile.

Inputs (each contributing to the composite):
1. **HPPH** (Hygiene Production Per Hour)
2. **CCDR** (Chairside Co-Diagnosis Rate)
3. **RAR** (Reattendance Rate)
4. **Same-Day Acceptance %**
