# How To Use This Book

This is not a "read once and feel inspired" book.
It is an operating manual.

If you use it that way, it will change your numbers, your handoffs, your retention, and your team culture.
If you read it passively, it will sit on a shelf looking motivational.

Use it actively.

## Use the Workbook Tools Correctly

At the end of each chapter you get tools.
Do not try to run all tools at once.

Use this rule:

- one chapter
- one tool
- one owner
- one deadline

Most implementation fails from overload, not lack of insight.

If a tool works, standardize it.
If it does not, adjust and retest.
Do not abandon the chapter after one rough week.

## Track Before You Change

Before implementing anything major, capture baseline:

- **HPPH (Hygiene Production Per Hour)**: total hygiene production divided by total hygiene chair hours.
- **CCDR (Chairside Co-Diagnosis Rate)**: percent of hygiene visits that generate at least one doctor-side treatment plan.
- **SDA (Same-Day Acceptance)**: percent of hygiene-originated findings accepted/scheduled on the same day.
- **RAR (Reappointment/Reattendance Rate)**: percent of patients who return for their next scheduled hygiene visit.
- **Exam Delay Average**: average minutes between requested doctor exam and doctor arrival.
- **Missed Break/Lunch Frequency**: number of skipped breaks/lunches per hygienist per week.

If you do not baseline, you cannot prove improvement.
And if you cannot prove improvement, momentum dies.

## Suggested Team Roles During Implementation

- **Clinical Lead / Hygiene Lead**: owns chapter-by-chapter rollout
- **Doctor Owner / Clinical Director**: owns decision support and exam choreography
- **Ops / Front Desk Lead**: owns scheduling, follow-up, and tracking integration
- **Cadence Owner**: ensures meetings, audits, and score reviews happen on time

No owner = no implementation.

## What to Expect in the First 90 Days

Most teams see this pattern:

### Days 1-30

- language feels deliberate and slightly awkward
- handoffs become clearer
- hidden workflow friction surfaces

### Days 31-60

- conversion and clarity improve
- less doctor re-explaining
- better continuity at checkout

### Days 61-90

- KPI trend starts moving reliably
- team confidence increases
- leadership conversations shift from "why aren't we growing?" to "how do we scale safely?"

Progress is usually not linear week to week.
Trend quality matters more than a single day.

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. trying to implement four chapters in one month
2. treating scripts as memorization instead of behavior design
3. changing schedule models before handoff quality is stable
4. using metrics to punish instead of develop
5. skipping mentorship cadence because "we are too busy"

Busy is exactly why systems are needed.

## Your Rule for the Entire Book

At the end of every chapter, ask:

3. How will we know in 14 days if it worked?

Write the answers down.

That is how this book moves from insight to outcomes.
