# Chairnomics Terminology

This appendix defines the core coined terms used throughout this playbook.

---

### Chairnomics
The overall brand and operating philosophy: hygiene-led growth through better conversations, better choreography, and better continuity.

### The C.H.A.I.R. Method
The five-phase clinical method used inside the appointment:
- Connect
- Hear & Hunt
- Assess & Articulate
- Invite
- Reinforce & Re-Engage

### The A.P.E.S. Method
The four-phase implementation method used at the practice level:
- Assess
- Plan
- Engage
- Sustain

### The Golden 50
The standard hygiene appointment window, treated as a high-value relationship and decision interval rather than a routine cleaning slot.

### Chairside Equity
The trust a hygienist accumulates over repeated visits, which increases acceptance, retention, and referral strength over time.

### The Trust Window
The first 90 seconds of the appointment, when the patient decides whether this visit feels transactional or relational.

### Word Leaks
Habit phrases that unintentionally reduce urgency, trust, or treatment follow-through.

### The Conversation Conversion Ladder
The sequence used to move from observation to patient understanding to informed action.

### The Invite Sequence
A structured handoff pattern that preserves patient agency while improving decision clarity and same-day scheduling.

### The Double Hygiene Decision Matrix
A quadrant framework used to choose whether and how to run double, assisted, or accelerated hygiene safely.

### The Hygiene Health Score
A composite score combining production, diagnosis quality, continuity, and acceptance to track overall hygiene system health.

### The Mentor Cadence
A recurring development rhythm (daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly) used to reduce drift, protect team stamina, and sustain performance.

### HPPH (Hygiene Production Per Hour)
Total hygiene production divided by total hygiene chair hours.

### CCDR (Chairside Co-Diagnosis Rate)
Percent of hygiene visits that produce at least one doctor-side treatment plan.

### SDA (Same-Day Acceptance)
Percent of hygiene-originated findings accepted and scheduled on the same day.

### RAR (Reappointment/Reattendance Rate)
Percent of patients who complete their next scheduled hygiene appointment in the target period.

### Exam Delay Average
Average minutes between requested doctor exam and doctor arrival.

### Missed Break/Lunch Frequency
How often hygienists miss protected breaks or lunch; a critical operations and burnout indicator.
