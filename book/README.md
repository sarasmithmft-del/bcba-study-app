## Author / Voice Frame

- **Author**: Sara Smith, RDH
- **Voice**: Sara writes as an **experienced hygienist** — someone who has sat in 200+ hygiene operatories, studied the patterns, and built The C.H.A.I.R. Method to interrupt them. Not as a memoirist telling her own clinical story.
- **Stories are composites** drawn from implementation and chairside work. Each chapter notes which characters are composite. Real, de-identified cases can be swapped in as Sara prefers.

## Locked Brand

- **Brand**: Chairnomics
- **Clinical Methodology**: The C.H.A.I.R. Method
- **Implementation Methodology**: The A.P.E.S. Method (Assess. Plan. Engage. Sustain.)
- No `[BRAND]` placeholders remain in book drafts.

## Workbook Tools

Each chapter ends with workbook tools. In the final designed book, these will be set off visually (different paper stock, tear-out pages). In these drafts they appear inline at the end of each chapter, clearly demarcated.
